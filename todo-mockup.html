Option A: Sticky Date Headers

Tasks grouped by relative time periods (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This Week, etc.) with sticky headers that stay visible as you scroll through each section.

  • Immediate temporal awareness - scan urgency at a glance
  • Familiar pattern (iOS Reminders, Todoist)
  • Minimal design change from current
  • Sticky headers provide context while scrolling

Option B: Left Border Accents

Same grouping as Option A, but with colored left borders on each task indicating urgency level. More subtle visual differentiation.

  • Color-coded urgency visible on each task
  • Clean, minimal design
  • Works well with dark mode
  • Less visual noise than sticky headers

Option C: Mini Week Strip

A horizontal scrollable week view at the top showing task counts per day. Tapping a day could filter or scroll to that section. Includes an overdue banner.

  • Strongest "calendar feel"
  • Quick week overview at a glance
  • Interactive - tap days to navigate
  • Overdue tasks prominently called out

Option D: Current Flat List

Your existing design - a flat list sorted by date. Each task shows its due date inline. No temporal grouping.

  • Simple and familiar to your current users
  • Compact - shows more tasks at once
  • Less visual complexity
  • BUT: No quick way to see "what's due today?"

Option E: Date Cards

Each date gets its own card with a calendar-style date badge. Most "calendar-like" feel - mimics looking at individual calendar days.

  • Strongest visual hierarchy per day
  • Calendar-style date badges are familiar
  • Clear separation between days
  • Great for days with few tasks each
  • BUT: More vertical space usage

Option F: Unified Card with Internal Sections

One seamless card with the add input as the hero at the top. Date sections are internal dividers within the card - preserving the "writing on a notepad" feel while adding temporal structure.

  • Add input stays as the hero - first thing you see
  • One unified card surface - seamless scrolling
  • Preserves "writing on the card" feel
  • Minimal visual change from current design
  • Section dividers add structure without fragmenting UI
  • Compact - no wasted space between sections
